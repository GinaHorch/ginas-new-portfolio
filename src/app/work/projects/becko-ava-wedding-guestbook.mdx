## Context

Becko & Ava's Wedding Guestbook is an interactive Progressive Web App built for real wedding guests to share messages, photographs and videos during and after the couple's wedding. Instead of a physical guestbook, guests open the app on their phones — access is QR-code-compatible — leave a personal message, upload media, and browse the memories other guests have contributed.

It also includes an authenticated administration area so submissions can be reviewed and moderated.

The live application is public. The repository and all real guest and admin data are private.

## Design credit

**The visual design and identity, including the custom illustrations and the UX direction, were created by Bianca Di Biase.**

## The interesting problem: a wedding is a bad time for a slow upload

Guests use this on their phones, on whatever connection they have, during and after a wedding. Each submission allows up to **five photographs and one video**, and phone cameras produce large files.

Compressing images in the browser before they are transferred keeps oversized uploads from reaching storage, and keeps submissions workable on a phone connection — without asking guests to think about file sizes at their friends' wedding. It is a small piece of engineering that decides whether the feature is usable at all in the conditions it actually runs in.

## Data and moderation

The administration area lets authorised users view guest messages, review photographs and videos, approve or reject submissions, download media and moderate content — behind authentication and protected routes.

Guest submissions are personal content belonging to real people, so nothing from the production application appears in this case study. Screenshots here show public-facing UI only; no guest messages, photographs, videos, personal details or admin data are published.
